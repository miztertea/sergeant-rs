`tdd`, `docs/gauntlet/contracts/N1.md`) for test-driven development on one
confirmed seam at a time: red, green, one minimal implementation. Use when:
a feature or bug fix is being implemented test-first.

pinned stage order. Full behavior-unit citations are archived at
`docs/gauntlet/promoted-provenance/tdd.md`; the curation act itself is
